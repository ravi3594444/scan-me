# Sound effects

Every effect is an unmodified Mixkit preview file, downloaded from assets.mixkit.co. They are used under the
[Mixkit Sound Effects Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/#sfxFree), which covers commercial and personal
projects and needs no attribution. They are credited here anyway.

`tools/mix.py` places each one by its measured peak or onset, and sets its level by measurement. Some cues change the playback rate or fade the tail.
